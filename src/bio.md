I'm currently a Staff Software Engineer at [Headway](https://headway.co/), working on building tools to empower mental health providers. Previously I spent a lot of years at Amazon helping packages get to your door as fast as possible.

I care a lot about building software that matters, and building it with teams that prioritize trust, empathy, and ownership. I enjoy finding the simplest way to solve complex problems, leaning new things, and not taking things too seriously.

In the real world, my wife and I live in Seattle with a kid and a dog. I love to get outside as much as possible - climbing, hiking, biking, whatever.
